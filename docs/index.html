Broof

Your phone is the distraction. This is the study bro.

What it is

A small box that quizzes you. Gemini writes questions on any topic or on your own notes. You answer by flipping a real switch. Lights and an LCD tell you if you were right, and a voice explains why.

Hands-on

Four switches are your answers A to D.

Your notes

Paste a lecture, get a quiz on exactly that.

No skipping

Questions you miss come back.

Built at MakeUC 2026

Arduino UNO Q, Gemini API, Python, C++.
